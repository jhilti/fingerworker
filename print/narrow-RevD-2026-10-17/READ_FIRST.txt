FINGERWORKER NARROW REV D — 17 OCTOBER 2026

Overall frame width: 158 mm (previously 180 mm).
Grip deck width: 104 mm (previously 125 mm).
Clear finger pocket width: 92 mm (previously 94 mm).
Spring/rod axes: Y +/-64 mm; 30 mm operating travel and purchased spring sizes retained.
Base remains 6 mm thick. Sensor, central slide and electronics enclosure dimensions are retained.

Use P01, P02, P04 and P05 from this revision together. The older wide parts and
their mounting patterns are incompatible with this assembly. Rear retainer and
return-pad shapes remain unchanged; their assembled positions move inward.
Route the sensor connector inward and retain slack in the real sensor cable.

The four optional bench mounts are now X20/Y+/-10 and X127/Y+/-39 mm.
Use flush 90-degree M5 countersunk heads, maximum head diameter 10 mm.
Rear mounting heads must remain at or below base top Z4 to clear the carriage Z5.
These bench fasteners are optional external mounting hardware, not assembly screws.

These are review STL exports, not a newly sliced Bambu project. Choose suitable
print orientation/supports and check the revised parts in the slicer. Existing
Rev F 3MF projects still contain the older wide P01/P02/P04/P05 geometry.

Hand fit remains provisional: narrowing reduces measured sample overlap, but
the yoke still conflicts with the scan. Assemble without springs for the first
physical fit check. No strength or loaded-use approval follows from this revision.
