# Two S01 spring spacer prototypes

Open **S01_22mm_spacers_H2D_04mm_PLA.3mf** in Bambu Studio. It contains **two identical copies at 100% scale**, prepared for **H2D · 0.4 mm · PLA · textured PEI**.

Saved settings: **0.20 mm layers, 4 walls, 100% rectilinear infill, no supports**, broad flat roots down and pilots upward. Estimate for both: **42 min 29 s / 9.36 g PLA**.

Each adapter has a **Ø16 × 22 mm body**, **Ø11.4 × 2 mm pilot** and **Ø6.6 mm bore**. Overall height is 24 mm; the spring shoulder provides 22 mm of axial spacing. An individual printable STL is in [print/](print/S01_spring_spacer_22mm.stl).

Use these with the **new 0X-RDF2127 springs**, following [spring selection and assembly instructions](ASSEMBLY_AND_SPRING_SELECTION.md). Keep the steel seats and guide rods. The flat root bears on the fixed steel seat; the pilot faces into the spring. Remove the spacers when reverting to the original springs or using the isometric sleeves.

Check the bore/pilot fit, flat seating and full free travel on the real parts. These are prototype adapters; their strength, creep and cycle life are unqualified. They do not establish a 500 N device rating.
