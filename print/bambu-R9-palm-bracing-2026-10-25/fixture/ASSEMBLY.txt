Q01_UNLOADED_TEST_FIXTURE — R8 hand-fit and manual-motion prototype only

Keep the R8 product CAD unchanged. Q01 fixes the nominal p=0 yoke datum for
an unloaded, supported fit study. It is not a sensor, preload adjuster,
force-bearing metal replacement or calibration device. Apply no springs,
isometric load or 500 N test force. The product strength target remains
unqualified.

OMIT THE REAL SENSOR/PRELOAD STACK while Q01 is installed:
H01 TE force sensor; H02 steel pusher; H11 preload square nut; H12 steel
sensor cassette; H13 preload screw/thumbwheel; H15 keeper; both H16 keeper
screws and H17 keeper nuts; both H18 cassette retention screws and H19
preload-limit spacers; H20 pusher nut; H21 preload lock screw and H22 brass
pad; H23 front sensor keeper; all four H24 spacers and H25 keeper screws.
Also omit both H03 springs, both H14 isometric sleeves and the H05/H06
spring-seat hardware. Never add any real sensor-stack parts around Q01.

Insert Q01 into the empty P01 portal from the open front (+X): the 12 mm
square plug enters the 12.4 mm square pocket, and the diameter18 flange
rear face seats on portal material at X=-16. With the rods aligned and
the rear caps/carriage clear as necessary, slide P05 from +X onto the
plain diameter3 tail until its pusher face meets the shoulder at X=1.
Install the remaining unloaded guides/carriage/rear assembly according
to the fit-test instructions. Keep the fixture and yoke supported.

The square plug and tail locate radially; neither is an axial latch and
neither has a functional printed thread. Maintain only gentle hand
contact in the normal compression direction. Do not lift the assembly
by Q01 or pull the yoke off its plain tail. Set p=0 only. With both rear
screws set equally to a=0..2 mm, manually guide the carriage through
u=0..(30-a) mm. This checks nominal fit/reach/motion, not sensor response,
switch timing qualification, spring preload, force transfer or strength.

To remove Q01, unload/support the assembly and slide P05 toward +X clear
of the plain tail. Withdraw Q01 toward +X out of the portal. Q01 must be
fully removed before installing the real sensor/preload stack.

PRINT AND TOLERANCES:
Use Q01_UNLOADED_TEST_FIXTURE_print-mm.stl at 100 percent scale. It is
already oriented with the square plug down and the fixture X axis vertical
(18 x 18 x 28 mm nominal). PLA, 0.2 mm layers, 4 walls, 100 percent infill
and a 3 mm brim are a suitable fit-test starting point. Allow normal
supports also ON MODEL: supports from the bed under the flange underside
at print Z=4.5, and accessible supports from the flange top under the small
shoulder at Z=20.5. The lower flange shadows the shoulder, so buildplate-only
supports cannot reach that shoulder. Clean the portal flange datum flat
without shortening the part; the forward shoulder bearing face is upward
and does not need support. Remove brim and seam bulges from fitting faces.

Nominal locator clearance is 0.2 mm per square-pocket flat; the tail has
0.1 mm radial clearance in the P05 bore. These are CAD clearances, not a
printer-tolerance guarantee. Stop if the plug/tail needs force; check and
lightly deburr support/seam artifacts before assembly. Do not sand either
axial bearing face to make a fit. Printed guide rods are shape proxies;
their roundness and friction do not represent ground steel rods.
