# R9 — one-plate hand-fit prototype

Use **H2D · 0.4 mm · PLA · textured PEI · 100% scale**. The full project has **19 pieces**: 12 rigid R9 parts, four hard P07 pad proxies, two Ø6 × 141 mm rod proxies and Q01. Real steel rods can replace the printed proxies. This set checks size, reach and manual motion; its hard pads do not reproduce TPU cushioning.

For an existing R8 assembly, use the separate **P02 wrist-down reprint project**. All other parts and hardware remain. P02 prints with its wrist ledge down and its new bearing faces upward.

## Assemble

1. Remove brims/supports and seam bulges, especially from the P04 guide, P02 mounting slots, P06 sockets/guides and P12 slots. Keep blind-hole floors and Q01's two bearing faces intact. Check actual heat-set inserts against the pilots before installation; see [hardware and detailed steps](ASSEMBLY_DETAILS.md).
2. **Omit the sensor/preload stack, springs, spring seats and isometric sleeves.** Insert Q01 from the finger side into the empty P01 portal until its square plug/flange seats. With rods aligned and rear ends accessible, slide P05 onto the rods and Q01's plain tail until it gently meets the shoulder. Fit P04 over the rods/central guide and fit P11. Q01 has no axial latch; keep everything supported.
3. Assemble each P12/P06 rear unit with its real nut, reaction washer and adjuster; fit its M2 capture screw and square nut before closing the cap onto P01. Fit the rear M3 screws. Attach two P07 proxies to each shoe with thin removable adhesive. Set both rear screws equally at **a=0** and lock the jam nuts while counterholding the adjusters; bench setup with caps removed is easiest for this trial.
4. Before tightening the palm, check that **both new flat pads seat gently and evenly against the fixed frame wall**, clear of the floating yoke, sensor space and lid. Remove support/seam artifacts; never pull an interfering palm into place with its bolts. Fit the palm and empty enclosure. Close the lid before fastening the case under the palm; countersunk lid heads must be flush. No PCB, battery, switch or force sensor is included in this print set.

## Measure and try

- Q01: **12 mm square plug, Ø3 mm tail, 17.0 mm between bearing faces**. Nominal plug clearance is 0.2 mm per flat; tail radial clearance 0.1 mm. Deburr artifacts; never force the fits or shorten the datum faces.
- P12 tongue **6 × 3 mm**, guide **6.6 × 3.6 mm**, capture slot **2.2 mm**. All must move freely. P07 proxies are **1.50 mm** thick; adhesive adds thickness. Do not drive the adjusters past **0–2 mm**.
- Palm/lid gap: **0.6 mm nominal**. A **0.5 mm feeler** should pass between the clean lid and ledge. Existing mounting collars touch the palm intentionally.
- Finger opening: **72 mm**. With p=0, expect **30 mm** stroke at a=0 or **28 mm** at equal a=2. Palm curved-tangent to finger-wall reference span: **109.25 → 79.25 mm** at a=0; **107.25 → 79.25 mm** at a=2. Measure from the curved contact, not the lower ledge edge.
- Run **10 slow, supported, unloaded cycles**. Record friction/skew, equal rear settings, hard edges/pinch points, palm comfort and reach. Photograph rest and the comfortable closed position.

Keep only gentle seating contact. **No squeezing against stops, springs, preload, force measurement or 500 N testing with Q01/printed rods.** Remove Q01 completely before installing the real sensor stack.
