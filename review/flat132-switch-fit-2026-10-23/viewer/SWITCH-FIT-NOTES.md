# R4 switch-fit review notes

R4 retains the R3 half-round palm, its 7 mm reach reduction, the 72 mm clear finger opening and the R2 printed reinforcement. The switch-fit revision moves the switch, mounting features and manual cam by **+4 mm X, +1 mm Y and +1.5 mm Z**. Native regeneration and nominal geometry validation are complete; physical qualification remains pending.

The BOM part remains **Omron SS-01GL2**, with hinge roller and solder terminals. The switch shape is reconstructed from the [Omron SS-series manufacturer drawing](https://omronfs.omron.com/en_US/ecb/products/pdf/en-ss.pdf), pages 4 and 6. It is not an Omron STEP model. The exact TE FX1901 force-sensor manufacturer STEP remains in the assembly.

The switch representation includes the 19.8 × 10.2 × 6.4 mm main housing, 9.5 mm mounting-axis spacing and conservative dimensioned terminal/lug envelopes. The terminal holes, corners and full projecting-lug profile are unresolved. H09a/H09b remain an illustrative lever and roller mechanism. The drawing does not supply enough geometry to qualify the real hinge/plunger motion from this proxy.

The original R3 installation omitted the terminal envelopes. An independent Boolean check of the R3 native meshes found that the three terminal boxes encroach on P05 once preload exceeds 1.1 mm. At 2 mm preload the axial penetration is 0.9 mm, with 1.44 mm³ intersection per rectangular envelope. Those values describe envelopes rather than exact factory metal volume.

The +4 mm axial move changes nominal terminal-tip X from 10.1 to 14.1 mm. Against the P05 reaction-web face at X = 9 + preload, the nominal axial gap becomes **5.1 − preload mm**, or **3.1 mm at 2 mm preload**. The supplied native geometry confirms the relocated terminal extent. Nominal geometry validation covers nearby stops, pedestal and the complete legal stroke/preload domain. The Y/Z moves require the matching pedestal, inserts and cam to follow the switch. An unchanged housing dimension does not establish correct installation by itself.

COM/NC use does not permit omission of the unused NO terminal from packaging. Solder fillets, wire insulation, strain relief and bend radii require their own allowances. The current M2/printed-insert mounting arrangement also requires its own clamping verification; the manufacturer's M2.3 mounting torque cannot be transferred to it.

After each preload change the H10 cam offset remains manually set to minus preload relative to its zero-preload setting. There is no automatic linkage. Real NC opening, remaining actuator travel, fastener retention and wire routing require physical checks. The 500 N value remains an unverified design target; qualified NCLOW force measurement remains blocked in the existing firmware.

The viewer uses genuine native R4 exports, bound to source SHA-256 `b150ff3a76cfaaaaa65ee1ea09e144b7c7d64e45459a925163e56918f48bb3c5` and the [immutable R4 Onshape version](https://cad.onshape.com/documents/15316cc6370631845af136b2/v/1a51bc54c29fb727dcb77016/e/d253eb87b70818ebde8215f0). The previous R3 immutable link is a baseline only.

The [final validation report](../validation/validation.json), SHA-256 `ed72872484b09ceb5560b1cf01476bbc1b46071fc165cf75ee2b8689563b89f7`, records nominal geometry PASS with zero failed or unresolved checks: three native configurations, the legal motion/service domain and 105 valid STEP solids. The six changed references match the exact CSG study; 99 other bodies are unchanged. That pass applies to the represented nominal geometry, including the conservative drawing envelopes. It does not qualify the real switch actuator, total travel, cam reset/lock, wiring or physical load rating.

The original hand scan is unchanged, with its previously accepted approximate placement. It is not rescaled, articulated or used to claim physical comfort/fit. The opening remains 72 mm.

## Rebuild

Update `poses-input.json` with the supplied source hash, geometry-origin/provenance, exact archive hashes, STEP hash and any immutable version/package. Never substitute R3 exports for missing R4 exports. The builder refuses to run without at least one hash-bound supplied configuration.

Run `build_viewer.py` with `/tmp/fingerworker-sensor-cad-20261022/bin/python`. It embeds local Plotly and all display geometry. Only the two indicative spring display meshes may be simplified; production geometry is not edited. Native/local poses are supplied inputs, not generated by the viewer.

Use `check_viewer.py` for source binding and JavaScript syntax checks, then perform browser QA separately. A successful static check does not constitute CAD clearance validation or browser QA.
