# Flat132 R6 — print and assembly

Print **P02 as one rigid part** in place of both the R5 P02 core and P03 TPU half-shell. Do not fit an old palm sleeve over the new block: the new P02 already has the final 10.25 mm outer half-round profile. The wrist contact and flat back stay at X−37.25 and X−27 mm respectively. Its palm plate, keyed mounts and electronics pilots are retained. The existing electronics tray/lid remain; there is no extended cover in R6.

The print set contains **14 Pxx parts**: P01, P02, P04, P05, P060/P061, P070a/P070b/P071a/P071b, P08, P09, P10 and P11. Only the four P07 bumper parts are intended to remain flexible. The P07 pads stay 1.5 mm thick; the R5 rigid rear-return extensions supply the closer rest. The 52 device STLs and 14 print STLs are unsliced native millimetre geometry in original neutral assembly coordinates. Orient/support parts in the slicer. A single closed CAD solid does not require 100% infill; choose filament, perimeters, infill and layer orientation for a tested process. No material/process is qualified for 500 N here.

Use the existing palm mounting hardware and keyed mount interfaces. There are eleven nominal insert locations in the whole assembly (two M4, five M3, four M2); none is a separately modeled insert. The actual heat-set insert OD, length and recommended pilot remain unknown and must be matched to stock. Keep the steel force-path nuts/keepers. Physical hard-palm comfort, fastener fit, print accuracy and palm stiffness must be checked on the prototype.

The rest of the coordinated R5 mechanism remains: P01 extended front guide, P04 cam well, P05 broad integral sensing stops, P060/P061 rigid return bosses, and P11 shoe clamped by the vertical H10 M3×10. The P05 central web and 14×22 mm stop faces remain. User travel is `u=0…30−preload`; absolute carriage displacement is `q=5+u`. The selected isometric setup uses **two 81 mm steel sleeves**, nominal Ø16/Ø8 mm, in place of both springs. Do not print these sleeves or mix lengths. Isometric seating is `q=20−preload`, or `u=15−preload` from the closer rest.

The old horizontal R4 cam bore is absent. P11 is manually set to `camTrim − preload`, with trim bounded to ±0.8 mm, then clamped. Reset after preload changes and physically calibrate trip, release and overtravel. Remove H10 completely before P11 service; slide the shoe 9 mm wristward, then lift it 25 mm. It does not lift through the roof slot. Only service unloaded and with spring energy restrained or removed; current validation records the exact service paths and removals.

The new palm changes cushioning. The R5 spring pretension and initial measurement dead zone remain: light-pair rest force about 39.7–52.9 N, stronger pair about 66.8–81.6 N. The four return pads carry the initial reaction until hand force unloads them. Current firmware retains the NC-open force/peak block and is unchanged.

The Omron SS-01GL2 remains a manufacturer-drawing clearance reference with illustrative lever/roller motion, not a factory STEP. Keep all three terminal envelopes clear and allow actual solder, insulation, wire bends and strain relief. The genuine TE FX1901 manufacturer solid remains unmodified. Printed load capacity, creep/fatigue, calibration, real switch fit, insert retention and hand comfort remain physical qualification tasks.
