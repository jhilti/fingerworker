# Actual FX1901 mount proposal, 22 October 2026

Frozen source: `mount-proposal.fs`, revision v3, SHA256 `ebc9b31fa760c11fdcf182fd88e378abae6babac2ca214b169f0bf072b6f8d50`. This is an implementation proposal for root integration, not a separate manufacturing release. It intentionally retains old H01 proxies until root replaces them with the genuine derived body query.

The manufacturer's unchanged sensor remains at apex X−1.9, Y0, Z18. Only the mount changes. H12 is a machined S355 steel 30 × 30 × 2.15 mm backing plate. Its front X−9.85 supports the actual planar rear surface, with no old centre relief. The shallow interrupted collar locates the housing with 0.25 mm radial clearance. Its broad +Y notch clears the flared lead base, which extends beyond a circular housing envelope near Y11. A first narrow notch collided by 2.25 mm³; the final notch removed this collision in the local probe.

A removable 0.5 mm stainless keeper retains the sensor independently of spring preload. Four 8.45 mm steel spacers fix its rear face at X−1.4, leaving 0.5 mm ahead of the housing rim. Its Ø20 opening clears the central force contact. Clamp force closes through the spacers and backing plate. The keeper must remain unloaded during measurement; the intended force path remains the pusher → central sensor button → sensor rear surface → backing plate → M6 adjuster → fixed frame. The four M2 × 12 panhead screws have a 4 mm maximum head diameter and 1.6 mm maximum head height. The yoke has individual head reliefs with a 0.7 mm axial nominal gap. Keeper screws have 2.15 mm nominal steel thread engagement and 4.5 mm edge distance in the plate; keeper sheet holes have 4 mm edge distance.

The cassette guides move outward by 1.5 mm, retain their closed slot ends and receive a lead-height notch on +Y only. The two cassette retention screws move to X−12.8, Z8.5, away from the genuine lead base. The spacer is now OD4 / ID3.2 × 6 mm, and the existing M3 × 8 socket screw engages 2 mm of steel. A flat-bottom blind bore/bottoming thread to |Y|11.9 leaves 1.1 mm for runout before the screw tip at |Y|13. This is a light-retention interface; it is not the spring load path or a rated high-force stop. The screw tips clear the lower M2 keeper rods by 1.5 mm. Head-to-palm-foot clearance is 2 mm nominal.

The shallow frame pocket leaves a 4.7 mm floor. H12 extends to Z33, so the complete device is now 35 mm high from base Z−2, while its 132 mm width and 72 mm finger opening remain unchanged. The existing M6 adjuster, sensor apex, pusher shoulder reaction at X1, 0–2 mm preload and 0–30 mm dynamic stroke remain at their prior datums. All nine added keeper/rod bodies are included in synchronized preload translation.

The primary geometry checks are expected from the regenerated native source. `local-mount-probe/probe.json` provides supplementary zero-pose evidence: the genuine sensor and new mount parts have no unintended solid intersections; the four M2 nominal threaded engagements are explicitly classified. P01 in that probe is a reconstruction from the earlier native STL, with small joining-face strips differing from exact regenerated FS, so this does not replace native export verification. The final 2.15 mm steel-plate stiffness, printed frame/yoke strength, tolerances, the M3 blind thread and the 0.8 mm printed slot-end ligament need prototype qualification. No new load rating follows from collision removal.

See `mount-proposal.json` for authoritative coordinates, query names, material assumptions, clearances and fabrication notes. The finalization notes owned by the fit-audit agent supersede this internal proposal for the final deliverable.
