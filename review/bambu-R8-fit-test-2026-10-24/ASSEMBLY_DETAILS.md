# R8 — one-plate hand-fit prototype

Print the supplied Bambu project at **100% on an H2D, 0.4 mm nozzle, PLA, textured PEI**. It contains **19 pieces**: 12 rigid R8 parts, four hard P07 pad proxies, two Ø6 × 141 mm rod proxies and the separate Q01 fixture. Use real steel rods instead if available. The solid PLA pads and printed rods check shape and fit; they do not represent the friction or cushioning of the finished device.

## Prepare and assemble

1. Remove brims/supports completely, especially from the P04 guide, P02 mount slots, P06 rod sockets/tongue guides and P12 capture slots. Clean seams and elephant foot. **Do not enlarge holes by forcing a screw/rod through them.** Check actual insert OD/length against the pilots before heat-setting: M4 Ø5.2/L6 at the palm mounts, M3 Ø4.2/L4 at the four rear mounts and P11, M2 Ø3.2/L4 at the case mounts. Keep blind-hole floors intact.
2. Use real fastening hardware: two palm M4×10; four rear M3×8; one P11 M3×10; two rear M4×10 flat-point set screws, thin 7×7×2.2 mm square nuts, OD12/ID4.3×1 mm washers and AF7×2.2 mm jam nuts; two M2×8 capture screws with 4×4×1.2 mm square nuts. The empty case uses two M2×6; lid three M1.6×5 90° countersunk screws (heads ≤Ø3, flush); P10 two M1.6×3 pan screws. Insert/screw dimensions are nominal—use compatible actual stock.
3. Leave the **sensor/preload stack, springs, spring seats and isometric sleeves out**. With the rear ends of the guide rods accessible, insert Q01's square plug into the empty P01 portal from the finger side until its flange seats. Slide P05 along the aligned rods onto Q01's plain tail until it gently meets the shoulder. Slide P04 onto the rods/central guide and fit P11. Support the assembly throughout; Q01 has no axial latch. Never combine it with the real sensor stack.
4. Fit each P12 shoe into its P06 cap. Install its main nut, reaction washer and adjuster before closing the cap onto P01; retain the shoe with the M2 screw and square nut. Fit both rear caps and their four M3 screws. Attach the two P07 proxies to each shoe's flat pad positions with thin removable adhesive; measure the resulting pad stack, since adhesive increases its nominal 1.50 mm thickness. Set both sides equally at **a=0**, then lock their jam nuts while counterholding the adjusters. Bench adjustment with caps removed is simplest for this unloaded test.
5. Fit the palm and the **empty** case/lid. Fit the lid before attaching the case under the palm. Keep its countersunk heads flush. This print set contains no PCB, battery, switch or force sensor.

## Check and record

- Q01: **12 mm square plug**, **Ø3 mm plain tail**, **17.0 mm between the two axial bearing faces**. The nominal clearances are 0.2 mm per plug flat and 0.1 mm around the tail. Deburr artifacts; do not shorten either bearing face.
- Rods: **Ø6 × 141 mm**; rear sockets Ø6.2. P12 tongue **6 × 3 mm** must slide freely in its **6.6 × 3.6 mm** guide; the **2.2 mm** slot must pass the M2 capture screw without binding. Do not force an adjuster beyond its 0–2 mm setting.
- Palm cover: nominal **0.6 mm** above P09. A **0.5 mm feeler** should slide between the clean lid and ledge; check that the case is not being clamped upward. Existing mounting collars touch the palm intentionally.
- Finger opening: **72 mm**. At a=0/p=0, manually slide P04 **30 mm** from rest; at equal a=2, expect **28 mm**. The reference span from the palm's curved tangent to the finger wall is **109.25 → 79.25 mm** at a=0, or **107.25 → 79.25 mm** at a=2. Measure from the curved palm contact, not the lower ledge edge.
- Run **10 slow, supported, unloaded cycles**. Record guide friction, left/right skew, rear setting equality, hard edges/pinch points, palm comfort and reach. Keep only gentle contact to seat Q01; do not squeeze against the stops. Photograph the hand position at rest and the comfortable closed position.

This is an unloaded size/motion trial. **No springs, preload, force measurement or 500 N test with Q01 or the printed rod proxies.** Remove Q01 before installing the real sensor stack.
