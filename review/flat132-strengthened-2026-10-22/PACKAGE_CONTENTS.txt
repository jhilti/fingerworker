The native/STEP file contains the complete neutral assembly including purchased hardware and electronics reference envelopes. Those reference bodies are not printable parts. The hand scan and interactive viewer are excluded from this print-update ZIP. The original hand remains an approximate reference in the live viewer.

Read README.md and PRINT_NOTES.md for revised service paths, 30-minus-preload travel and manual click-cam setup. 500 N is a design target, not a verified rating. Current firmware still rejects qualified force at NC LOW; this CAD update does not alter that logic.
