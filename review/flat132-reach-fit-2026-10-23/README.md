# Flat132 R5 — 5 mm closer dynamic grip

R5 brings the finger carriage **5 mm closer to the palm at rest and throughout the dynamic stroke**, while keeping **30 mm travel at zero preload and 28 mm at 2 mm preload**. It moves the rear return contacts, shortens the distal P05 sensing stops, extends the front guide and replaces the horizontal cam screw with a top-clamped P11 shoe. The 132 mm frame width, 72 mm finger opening, 35 mm height and R3 half-round palm remain.

**Nominal CAD validation passes with zero failed or unresolved checks.** All 106 neutral STEP solids are valid; maximum travel, 81 mm ISO and both trim-limit witnesses pass. The independent source-equivalence certificate supports reuse of the original dynamic exports. The 500 N target still requires physical qualification. Frozen R4 remains unchanged.

The new rest adds 5 mm spring compression. Nominal starting force for the pair is approximately **40–53 N with the light springs**, or **67–82 N with the stronger springs**, across 0–2 mm preload. Minimum installed length at the dynamic stop is 66 mm, above both springs' catalogue operating-length limits. Tolerances, guidance and fatigue remain unqualified. There is an **initial measurement dead zone while the carriage remains against its return bumpers**: hand force first unloads that reaction, so the additional sensor reading alone does not establish the complete hand force in this interval.

The user-selected **81 mm ISO sleeves** bring the isometric grip **5 mm closer as well**. Approach measured from the new rest remains **15 mm at zero preload / 13 mm at 2 mm preload**. Use both 81 mm sleeves in place of the springs; the prior 86 mm option is preserved only as a checkpoint.

P11 is a manually adjusted switch cam, clamped by a vertical **M3×10** screw. Set its position to `camTrim − preload`; the bounded trim range is ±0.8 mm. After changing preload, reset and physically calibrate the shoe, then verify clamping, trip, release and overtravel. This is not automatic coupling. The fixed Omron SS-01GL2 remains a manufacturer-**drawing clearance reference**, with approximate lever/roller motion; the genuine TE FX1901 remains the unmodified manufacturer solid. Current firmware retains the NC force/peak block and is unchanged.
