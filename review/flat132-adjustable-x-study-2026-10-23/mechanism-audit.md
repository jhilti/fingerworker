# Read-only compact rear mechanism audit

Baseline is final R6; this is a proposal, not regenerated CAD or a clearance certificate.

The components near X120 are the P06 relaxed-return bosses. The 81 mm H14 steel isometric sleeves are separate and carry load through P05 and the sensor.

A coordinated 5 mm shortening is available without changing palm, pocket, springs, dynamic end position, switch cam or ISO hold: move rear retainers, feet, mount bolts, rod ends and rear bench holes 5 mm toward the palm; delete the 5 mm bosses; retain current return-pad front X119. Base length becomes151 mm and assembly X envelope175.1 mm. Rods become141 mm. Bench-hole shift X127 to122 is essential to retain the countersink rim.

For a useful adjustable rest, two recessed M4 set screws can position broad guided return shoes. An extra rest advance a consumes stroke directly. Keeping the existing sensor end stop and the user's28–30 mm travel requires a+preload<=2 mm, giving stroke30−a−preload. This is107.25–109.25 mm rest span with the existing initial-force envelope. Neither tiny knobs projecting behind the frame nor new metal heads may erase the5 mm envelope saving.

Corrected screw axes Y±51/Z10 avoid the original rear M3 vertical head bores. M4 shaft clearance to theØ6.2 rod socket is2.9 mm. A nominal7 mm square nut fits with1.4 mm top clearance to the rod socket,2 mm bottom wall and1.3 mm side gap to rear head counterbores. The earlier Y±58.5/Z18 concept intersects these rear head bores and is rejected.

A blind shoe cup with rigid front119.2−a and thrust face120.4−a gives a1.2 mm wall and0.2 mm setback behind the TPU pads. An8 mm set screw then ends128.4−a, within the shortened128.5 mm cap rear. Nominal3.2 mm captive nut at122.7..125.9 retains full engagement even ata2 with0.5 mm screw excess. A short cup alone loses guide overlap atfulla2: separate longer guide fingers must be engineered and validated around the nut and rear bolts. This is not a finished adjuster design.

Do not use a rear-frame screw as the isometric load stop: it bypasses the sensor. Adjustable ISO replacement would require two steel compression struts that still seat between P04 and floating P05. Ordinary solid screws cannot occupy the existingØ6 rod axes. No500 N rating is established.

These are proposed dimensions, not validation.
